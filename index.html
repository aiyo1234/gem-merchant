    📜 How to Play Gem Merchant

    Goal: Be the first chef to reach 15 Prestige Points to trigger the final round and win the game!

    On your turn, choose ONE action:

    • Take 3 Different Tokens: Pick 3 tokens of different gem colors from the bank.
    • Take 2 Identical Tokens: Pick 2 tokens of the SAME color (allowed only if bank has 4 or more available).
    • Purchase a Cuisine Card: Spend your tokens to acquire a dish. The card grants permanent prestige points and a permanent gem discount on future purchases!
    • Reserve a Card (Hold): Reserve 1 visible card into your private hand (max 3) and gain 1 Gold wild token (if available).

    Noble Patrons:

    If you accumulate enough permanent dish bonuses matching a Patron's requirements, they automatically visit you for +3 extra points at the end of your turn!

    Token Limit:

    You can hold a maximum of 10 tokens. If you exceed 10 at the end of your turn, you must discard the excess back to the bank.
